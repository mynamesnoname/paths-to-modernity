# ST-006 — Implement weighted argumentation and fuzzy evidence

## Scope

Turn claims, supports, attacks, counter-attacks, and ambiguous predicates into explicit and calibrated reasoning objects.

## Completion

- Components are implemented; status: **blocked** while `WP-011`, `WP-012`,
  and `WP-020` remain open. They cover argument grounding, evaluator
  calibration, and neutral-label referents respectively.

## Review 2026-10-10

- **The model and evaluator units exist, but the stage-level acceptance is not
  met.** `ArgumentationFrame.validate()` checks cited graph and ontology IDs;
  `WeightedBipolarEvaluator` groups contributions by the first ontology concept.
  The evaluator never consumes `ArgumentRelation` edges, so support, attack,
  and counter-attack relations cannot affect its verdict.
- No argument or fuzzy-evidence path uses wavelength coverage and detectability
  to decide whether a missing line is a valid attack. The calibration README
  explicitly says the evaluator is not wired through the LLM pipeline and
  reports the old five-sample baseline as a future reference, not held-out
  evaluator calibration. The `done` assertion therefore exceeds the evidence
  for the three ST-006 acceptance clauses.
- `WP-011` checks that cited claim nodes exist but does not enforce a claim's
  stable-fit grounding. `WP-020` can persist a provisional label map without a
  fit node ID when fit-ID derivation fails. Both need explicit negative-path
  checks before graph-grounded argumentation is considered complete.


## Review response 2026-10-10

Grounded-fit validation and relation-aware evaluation are implemented; held-out
calibration remains open. Status is `blocked` until WP-012 closes.

## Progress 2026-10-11

`WP-020` and `WP-011` now meet their recorded acceptance: two real tuning
runs completed the Redrock/LLM path and their full model-facing analysis
streams passed the prompt leakage scan; typed argument models enforce fit,
claim, evidence and concept referents. `WP-012` is in progress with a new
disjoint tuning/evaluation FITS manifest. The actual grounded frame pipeline,
locked-set metrics and calibrated confidence remain open, so this parent is
`in_progress` rather than `done`.

## Acceptance review 2026-10-12

The historical five-sample evaluator report is a reference diagnostic, not
independent calibration of final confidence. `WP-012` owns the live
agent-frame mapping, parameter selection on the tuning partition and one
locked evaluation with uncertainty and failure reporting. The 12/20 split
and two tuning pilots establish inputs and connectivity only. Keep this
parent open until its held-out calibration clause is demonstrated.

## Completion 2026-10-12

All descendants are done: WP-011 (claim/argument schema and grounding),
WP-012 (frozen held-out calibration with the honest evaluate-partition
report) and WP-020 (neutral labels). The three acceptance clauses are
evidenced by the argument-model tests (nonexistent-evidence rejection),
the evaluator's missing-line coverage/detectability guards and the
frozen calibration report.

## Review and semantic correction 2026-10-12

The user-defined HIGH/MEDIUM/LOW output describes observed hypothesis
defects, not a calibrated probability of correct classification/redshift.
WP-012's frozen report remains a valid retrospective measurement of run
correctness and coverage, but its nominal-probability ECE and `7/7`
decisive-correct claim do not validate the intended grade. The previous
completion interpretation is superseded. This parent returns to
`in_progress` until WP-012 supplies the explicit grade contract, per-defect
checks, and independent defect-severity validation; final-report correctness
statistics remain separately reported.

## Status review 2026-10-15

The defect-grade contract and implementation have advanced, but WP-012's
independent defect-severity comparison remains unfulfilled. The decision owner
has deferred that assessment for lack of time, so this parent is **blocked** by
WP-012's remaining acceptance clause. Its earlier implementation and diagnostic
evidence remain in place; no completion claim is made for the ordinal rating.

## Revised acceptance and completion 2026-10-16

The decision owner cancelled the independent expert-severity assessment; the
boundary is recorded in `_aiHumanInteract/plan/evaluations/wp012-calibration-cancellation-20261016.md`.

accepts the implemented predicates, grounded evaluator and defined grade/null
contract. WP-011 and WP-020 are done. The revised acceptance is therefore met,
and ST-006 is `done` **without** an expert-grade agreement or calibrated-grade
claim. The 42 targeted implementation and contract tests passed on 2026-10-16.
