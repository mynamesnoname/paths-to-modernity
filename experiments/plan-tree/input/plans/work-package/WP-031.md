- **Absence stays an observation.** `negative_evidence` is true only when a context-specific finding (a WP-036 `rule_finding`) is applicable, definitive, negative, has every deciding operand measured and cites at least one measured graph node of its own. An unknown, partly masked or unusable window, an out-of-range prediction, an unresolvable line name, a missing mask array and a finding with an unmeasured operand all record `false` plus the reason in `not_negative_reason`. No universal expected-strength model or margin is used or required, so the decision owner's 2026-10-13 withdrawal of the parameter-freeze gate is respected rather than worked around.

`observed_feature:fixture`; the old gate accepted it as measurement. The gate now resolves the reference to an actual `observed_feature` node, checks the registered doublet relation and selected fit, and only accepts negative polarity. The cited feature must also be the kept claim for the *partner* component on that fit; a real but unrelated Mg II feature cannot suffice. The former fabricated positive test is now a refusal regression.
- A finding cannot turn a `MASKED`, out-of-range, partly usable, unverified or unresolved `NOT_FOUND` into negative evidence. The selected claim must itself assert `not_found`, and the full inspected window must be proven all usable. This intentionally means there is no asserted real positive negative-evidence case until a specific measured relation and complete mask are available.
