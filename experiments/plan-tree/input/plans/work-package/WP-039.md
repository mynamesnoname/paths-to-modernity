### from "Follow-up 2026-10-13 — the fallback belongs to the boundary"

The decision owner asked whether the degraded-labeling fallback could be moved out
of the shared helper and into the boundary.  It is now: - `HA.assign_neutral_hypothesis_labels` takes the ordering keys from its caller (`ordering_keys`, defaulting to the fit node IDs) and **raises** when a hypothesis has none.  It no longer invents a content-key fallback, so its contract is one key per hypothesis and nothing else.
- `VisualInterpreter.assign_boundary_labels` owns the policy: complete fit identity → strict labeling plus `require_complete_fit_label_map`; an unresolved `znum` → labels ordered by the recorded content key, map marked `fit_node_id_coverage: "incomplete"` with a `coverage_reason`, and the reason returned so the stage logs it.  Degrading is therefore explicit, recorded and visible in the synthesis caveat that already exists for incomplete maps.
- The harness stage's re-check is relaxed to "every labelled hypothesis has a map entry": completeness is the boundary's policy, not a second opinion here.
- Acceptance clause 2 above is updated to this contract; clause 4 replaces the "library-level fallback" wording.  Existing coverage/caveat tests still pass, and the suite gained the "library refuses to invent a key" case.
